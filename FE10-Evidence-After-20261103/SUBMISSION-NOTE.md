# FE-10 submission note

I audited the Home, Lace and Fabric & Style Advisor pages for mobile performance and accessibility. I added a keyboard-visible Skip to main content link, polite batched announcements for streamed Advisor answers, a shorter informative hero-image description, and eager loading for the above-the-fold Lace preview while keeping the larger dialog image on demand.

My clean deployed baselines were Home 79, Lace 83 and Advisor 80 for performance, with 100 accessibility on all three. After release, three Lighthouse 13.5.0 production runs per page produced performance/accessibility medians of Home 99/100, Lace 96/100 and Advisor 94/100. These before and after runs are different recorded environments, so I do not claim that FE-10 caused the score increase. A controlled GitHub-hosted released-source versus candidate comparison found identical performance medians—98, 96 and 99—so it established no measurable performance improvement.

The official WAVE extension reported zero errors, zero contrast errors and zero alerts on deployed Home, the open Lace dialog and the Advisor initial state. Local fixture-backed Advisor response and error states also scored 0/0/0. Keyboard-only checks passed for Skip to main, Home to Lace navigation, dialog open/Escape/focus restoration, Advisor controls and the 375px layout. With Windows Narrator and a deterministic local fixture, streamed advice was announced automatically, Stop prevented later stale batches, and the final short fragment was spoken. That was local fixture evidence, not Gemini or a production screen-reader test.

The production regression found no horizontal overflow or console errors and confirmed Bag Studio and the WhatsApp destination without sending a message. One benign production Advisor request exposed Stop and left the composer usable, but automation did not capture generated text, so I am not claiming production streaming was verified in that pass. Physical Safari also remains untested. Full methods, reports, screenshots, tradeoffs and limitations are in [AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md).
